## Decisions reflected in the tests (confirmed by the product owner on 2026-11-21)

- **R9:** "last 30 days, today included" = today and the 29 days before it (16 Oct is not active on 15 Nov).
- **R6:** a status change cannot be dated before the previous status change, and cannot repeat the current status.
- **US01-2:** domain exactly `up.pt` or `fe.up.pt`, any case; subdomains such as `edu.fe.up.pt` are rejected.

## Assumptions (wording, not business rules)

- **R2:** a median of exactly 7 days → "about 1 week".
- **R4:** a non-integer median → threshold rounded up (16.5 → 17), since days are whole numbers.
- **Consenting** = consent `true`; `NULL` (not answered) is not consenting (SPEC glossary).
